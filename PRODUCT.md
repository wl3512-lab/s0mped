# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in NYC (ages 18–30) who discovered s0mped on Instagram and want to book a tooth gem appointment. They're browsing on mobile, already sold on the aesthetic, and just need to understand the service and commit. Secondary user: someone who stumbled on the page from a friend's story and wants to see the work before deciding.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio and booking site for @s0mped, a mobile tooth gem artist in NYC. The site shows the work, explains the menu and pricing, displays available gems, and captures booking requests. Success looks like a visitor going from "what is this" to "I want one" to filling out the form — in a single scroll.

## Brand Personality

Polished, elevated, intimate. The work sits at the intersection of fine jewelry and dental cosmetics — premium crystals, applied safely, in your home. Confident without being loud. Feminine without being soft. New York without being cold. The brand has grown beyond its early roots into something that feels like a professional artist with a distinct point of view.

## Anti-references

- Generic spa/beauty salon sites (too safe, too pink)
- Dental office aesthetics (clinical white, green cross, sterile)
- Teen craft/DIY blogs
- Rhinestone cowboy kitsch
- AI-generated gradient blobs with no soul
- E-girl / alt TikTok aesthetic (too juvenile, too trend-chasing, no longevity)
- Sparkle overload (every element animated, sensory overwhelm, bling for bling's sake)

## Design Principles

1. **The work speaks** — photography and gem imagery carry trust more than copy does. Every layout decision should surface the actual crystals.
2. **Legibility over decoration** — the pricing must be crystal-clear. No one should leave confused about what they're paying.
3. **Mobile-first intimacy** — most visitors arrive from an Instagram tap on their phone. The site should feel like a natural extension of that story, not a website they had to "visit."
4. **Edit the sparkle** — restraint makes the iridescence more impactful. One well-placed shimmer beats ambient glitter everywhere. Sparkle earns its place or it doesn't appear.
5. **One scroll, one decision** — the page should resolve naturally into a booking action. No dead ends, no confusion about next steps.
6. **Polished artist, not polished brand** — the elevated feel comes from craft and restraint, not corporate sheen. It should feel like a person who takes their work seriously, not a brand that takes itself seriously.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. No specific known user needs beyond standard mobile accessibility. Reduced-motion support is required — all float/twinkle/shimmer animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
